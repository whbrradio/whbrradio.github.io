About WHBR Roanoke

WHBR Roanoke brings you unforgettable guitar riffs from '70s and '80s classic rock, along with the best punk rock hits from the '90s and early 2000s. From legendary rock anthems to high-energy punk favorites, we play the music you love.

Broadcasting from Roanoke, Virginia, WHBR brings great rock music to listeners everywhere, 24 hours a day, completely ad-free. No commercials, no interruptions — just real rock, without the ads!
